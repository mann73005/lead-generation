# Sample emails

Three emails generated by the running system from real discovered leads. Nothing
here is written by hand: every bracketed token in the Appendix A skeleton was
filled from a stored field, and the grounding report under each one shows which
fields, what was checked, and what the system refused to say.

The locked copy — the greeting, the four-sentence order, the sign-off — is
identical in all three. Only the tokens differ.

Generated 2026-10-02 against the live deployment.

## What to notice

**The P.S. is missing from all three.** `optional_soft_proof_point` has no
stored value — no customer proof point is on file — so Appendix A's rule applies:
omit it cleanly rather than fill it with generic filler. The line disappears
entirely rather than shipping as a bare "P.S.".

**No email address was found for any of them.** Leadership pages and trade-press
articles name people without publishing addresses. The system stores what it
reads and refuses to guess one. The lead is still ranked, and the send goes to
the sandbox inbox regardless.

**The signal sentence is the only part the model phrases**, and it is checked
before use: every number and proper noun in it must already appear in the stored
evidence. The capitalisation of company and person names is restored from the
stored values afterwards, because a model told to "start lower case" applies it
to the whole clause.

**Each email names a different pain point.** The match comes from what was
observed about the company and from the lead's own job title — a Head of Supply
Chain owns allocation whether or not any article mentions a stockout.
